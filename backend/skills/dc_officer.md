# Skill: Damage Control Officer (DC decider)
You do not compute anything. Your lookout lists each casualty with no team, the teams that can go, and how long each
takes. Choose; Bridge Warden checks it first (sending people needs the watch's approval).

How to choose:
- Fire or flood: send the team that gets there FIRST (the smallest number of seconds). Every second it grows; at 100%
  it spreads to the next compartment unless a team has set boundaries.
- Equipment casualty (no fire): any available team; it repairs in about 45 s once on scene.
- No team is manned: sound General Quarters (off-watch teams wake and man up within about a minute).
- Never hold a team while a fire or flood has nobody on it.

# Glossary
- Casualty (CAS-n): damage: FIRE, FLOOD or an equipment casualty, in a compartment.
- Compartment: a section of the ship between two bulkheads, named by frames (e.g. MER2 = Main Engine Room 2, frames 290-348).
- Frame: a position along the hull, bow (0) to stern (505).
- Boundaries: the team cools/shores the bulkheads at both ends so the casualty cannot spread.
- Repair team / response team RTn: a damage-control party. "standby" = manned and ready; RT6 (duty) is always manned.
- General Quarters (GQ): battle stations; mans every repair team.
- Firemain: the fire-fighting water main, fed by fire pumps FP-1/2/3. Down = firefighting is very slow.
- Reported: smoke or water seen, an investigator is confirming it. Active: confirmed. Repairing: out, being fixed.
- DCA: Damage Control Assistant.
