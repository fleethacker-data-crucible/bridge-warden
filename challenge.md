# Challenge: AI automated systems

> Official text from the event portal. Do not edit.

**Category:** Open Innovation

An unconstrained edge AI track for high-impact prototypes outside the curated use cases, under minimal power and bandwidth. Entries meet the Fleet-Ready Definition of Done and are judged on the same rubric.

## Overview
Future vessels and maritime environments are likely to rely on multiple AI-enabled and autonomous systems operating at the same time. While each individual system may perform well on its own, the combined behavior of multiple interacting systems can become difficult to predict, monitor and control. The challenge is not to create more autonomous agents nut rather an orchestration and governance layer that makes multi-agent systems more visible, bounded and manageable.

## Requirements
Develop a concept or prototype for a multi-agent governance and orchestration system. The solution should demonstrate how multiple onboard AI systems could share relevant information, coordinate actions, operate within defined authority limits, and remain subject to human oversight.

## Expected Deliverables
The solution could help answer questions such as:
- Which AI agents or autonomous systems are currently active?
- What information, tools, sensors or functions can each agent access?
- What actions is each agent authorized to recommend or perform?
- Where are recommendations, objectives or resource demands in conflict?
- How can the system detect unexpected, unsafe or non-compliant interactions?
- How can operators pause, override, constrain or roll back agent actions?

## Potential solution capabilities
- Agent identity, role and authority management
- Shared operational-context or "common operating picture" layers
- Communication protocols between autonomous systems
- Conflict detection between agent recommendations
- Priority, escalation and approval workflows
- Human-in-the-loop decision support
- Agent activity logging and explainability
- Monitoring of observable safety or operational constraints
- Resource and capability limits for individual agents
- Safe-stop, rollback or containment mechanisms
